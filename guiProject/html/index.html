Welcome to PRLog!

Your personal workout tracker. Track your sets, reps, and weights – get visual feedback on your performance, and more!

Get started

What PRLog can do for you:

Log Workouts

PRL is designed to make logging workouts easy. Simply add a workout, input your sets, reps, and weights lifted, then add it to your log. You can look back at and check out your past workouts.

Visual Graphs

We take your notes and go one step further by creating graphs of your workouts over time so you can track your progress, identify any sticking points, and reach your fitness goals.

Timers and Reminders

PRLog is your one stop shop for all of your workouts. Need a timer for your next circuit? We got you covered! How about a friendly reminder for your next workout? We can do that too.
